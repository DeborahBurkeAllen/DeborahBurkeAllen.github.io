Deborah M. Burke-Allen, RPh, is a registered pharmacist (NC #14013, issued under Deborah M. Allen) with 25+ years in pharmacy and healthcare. Her expertise is the interconnected body: how the gut, immune, nervous, endocrine, and metabolic systems influence one another, and how medications, nutrition, and supplements act across them. For a decade at an integrative medical practice, she built the clinical education and systems and mentored MDs, DOs, and NPs. Her clinical work included reviewing complex medication and supplement regimens and integrating functional and conventional labs into one clinical picture. She also led development and scaling of the practice's private-label supplement line: 25 formulations, about $360,000 a year, from formula selection and sourcing to final label approval. On the platform side, she managed the evaluation and migration from HelloHealth to Elation EHR and championed early adoption of Hint Health for membership billing. At Transformation Enzyme, she delivered national training for healthcare professionals, built education around recurring clinician knowledge gaps, and explained California Proposition 65 to practitioners. Earlier, she spent 15 years in high-volume pharmacy practice at Target. There, she opened a new store's pharmacy as its Pharmacy Manager, taught a continuing-education program across the Carolinas, and later served as pharmacist in charge on manager-off shifts. At Target and at the practice, she resolved prior authorizations and Medicaid claim rejections with prescribers and patients. Today she directs Claude Code to build applications and dashboards and verifies every AI-assisted claim against primary sources. Based in Charlotte, NC, she is seeking a full-time remote or hybrid role and is open to contract-to-hire.

Skills, with proof

Supplement Product, Quality, and Claims

  • Led development and scaling of a private-label supplement line of 25 formulations, about $360,000 a year, from formula selection and sourcing to Supplement Facts, DSHEA structure/function claims, and final label approval.
  • Selected and vetted manufacturers' existing formulations for minimal excipients, absorbability, and purity.
  • Worked with 3 manufacturers, reviewed their batch Certificates of Analysis and stability data, and kept lot records with expiration dates.
  • Identified and vetted alternate sources, comparing specifications and Certificates of Analysis, during a six-month backorder of the practice's top probiotic.
  • Took adverse event reports from families, reported each to the manufacturer, and checked whether the reaction was common or unusual.
  • Governed the portfolio by evidence: recommended against a switch the research did not support and documented a reformulation's likely efficacy loss, prompting the manufacturer to restore the original formula.

Clinical Evidence and Knowledge

  • Evidence grading: every claim rated established, plausible, or speculative, and traced to a verified primary source.
  • Literature synthesis: researched complex cases in PubMed, with UpToDate for clinical context; for a co-managed pediatric case, presented staged plans with full citations to the physician and family.
  • Lab integration and translation: GI-MAP, Doctor's Data, and Genova stool analyses, organic acids, and conventional labs read against optimal ranges as one clinical picture.
  • Medication expertise: prescription review for safety, interactions, and appropriateness, plus drug-nutrient and drug-supplement interactions.
  • Nutrigenomics: trained in genetic SNPs and epigenetics with Seeking Health (2017 to 2018) and presented StrateGene SNP reports.
  • Public health: reported Lyme disease and other reportable infections to the health department when lab results detected them.

Clinical Content and Education

  • Built the practice's public-, practitioner-, and patient-facing education, protocol guides, and decision-support resources.
  • Taught weekly family roundtables and led three twice-monthly mentorship cohorts for MDs, DOs, and NPs.
  • Converted families' repeated questions into standardized onboarding; at Transformation Enzyme, built national HCP training around recurring clinician knowledge gaps.
  • Created product information sheets and visuals, plus co-branded sheets and staff training for a partner office.
  • Wrote blog posts and a LinkedIn article; answered questions in a private Slack community and on social media.

Healthcare Technology and Platforms

  • Led the EHR migration from HelloHealth to Elation: evaluation, selection, account setup, pilot run, scheduling-team training, and clinical templates.
  • Requirements and integration: brought practice needs to Elation's technical team and coordinated outside lab and clinical data into the record.
  • Hint Health: championed early adoption, set up membership billing and supplement credits, and built the product catalog with storage and handling guidance at the point of use.
  • Paired automation with education after judging which tools helped and which created extra work when families were not informed.
  • Co-designed the membership enrollment, Zoom orientation, and structured intake (2016 onward) that enabled a rapid move to virtual care during COVID-19.

AI-Directed Building

  • Directs Claude Code to build applications and dashboards from written specifications, then evaluates the output.
  • Writes reusable AI quality rubrics with explicit pass and fail criteria for ATS readability and proofreading.
  • Uses Claude, ChatGPT, and Perplexity for deep research and verifies every citation against the primary paper.
  • Directed the build of this site and of a pipeline that generates tailored documents from one structured source.
  • Prototyped an AI-assisted lab dashboard that turns lab PDFs into trend data for practitioner review before any change.

Leadership and Operations

  • Opened the Stonecrest Target pharmacy as Supervising Pharmacist (Pharmacy Manager), 1998 to 2000, and led its Y2K readiness.
  • Directed technicians, workflow, staffing, and inventory as pharmacist in charge on manager-off shifts.
  • Earned certification to teach patient-counseling best practices and delivered a 6-hour CE program to pharmacists across Carolinas districts.
  • Resolved prior authorizations and Medicaid claim rejections from the pharmacy side, reviewing formulary alternatives with prescribers.
  • Served as the practice's escalation point for clinical and operations questions from staff, the health coach, and schedulers.
